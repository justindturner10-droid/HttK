# TCG Dungeon Runner

A hybrid Trading Card Game meets Roguelike Deckbuilder for Windows.

Combining the strategic depth of Yu-Gi-Oh!, the accessibility of Hearthstone, the addictive progression of Slay the Spire, and the deck-building mechanics of Dominion.

## Core Features (Planned)

- **Dynamic Deck Building:** Build and modify your deck during runs
- **Roguelike Progression:** Procedurally generated dungeons with permadeath
- **Strategic Card Combat:** Deep TCG mechanics with multiple card types
- **Meta-Progression:** Unlock new cards and abilities across runs
- **Challenging Encounters:** Varied enemies, elites, and epic boss battles
- **Relic System:** Powerful items that modify your runs
- **Multiple Paths:** Choose your own route through the dungeon
